My trades

Open a mailbox in this browser and read the trade mail from the last 10 days. Each fill is named as one of the trades on the study page, with the lesson’s reason for that shape and the trade it would switch to if the setup changed. The mail shows the fills. It does not show the motive.

Mailbox

Type any email address. The mailbox opens in a new tab, already searched for trade mail from the last 10 days. No broker key is used, and the address is not saved.

A way to read a position against the lesson. Not a recommendation to place a trade.
