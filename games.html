Five answers

Games

Five drills. Each one asks a different question from the study page: where a trade fits, what a desk calls it, what is actually in the account, and which trade you switch to when the setup changes.

  1. Falling answers

    A situation stays put. Three answers fall. Shoot the one that fits, or tap it, before the right answer reaches the ground.

  2. Pairs

    Match a desk’s name to the structure. Buy-write pairs with the covered call. A diagonal does not, so it is not in this deck.

  3. Tapes

    One trade falls. Tap the outlook it fits before it reaches the ground. The marks are the same five as the study page.

  4. The bench

    A ticket names a structure. Stamp only the parts that are actually in the account.

  5. Doors

    You already hold a trade. The setup changes. Pick the door before it closes.
